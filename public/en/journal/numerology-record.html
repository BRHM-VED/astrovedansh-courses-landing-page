Keep a reproducible numerology worksheet

AstroVedansh Journal·156 words

Updated: 17 October 2026

A useful worksheet records the date, selected convention, raw digits, intermediate totals and final number. For names, include the letter table as well. A teacher can then reproduce the calculation. An interpretation alone makes it difficult to identify where an error entered the process.

Day 23→5; full date 39→12→3; raw grid list 2,3,9,9,6.

A useful worksheet separates original information, the applied rule and the result. Hand it to another learner and ask whether they can reproduce the calculation without questioning you. If they need to ask which part of the year was included, the record is incomplete. Likewise, any calculated number added to a grid should have a labelled source. Write the interpretation afterwards. When something goes wrong, this structure helps identify whether the issue arose in the record, the addition or the interpretive step, rather than treating every disagreement as a mysterious exception.

Three different results are not contradictory when they answer three declared calculations.
