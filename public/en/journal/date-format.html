Resolve ambiguous dates before calculating

AstroVedansh Journal·160 words

Updated: 17 October 2026

Clarify the date format before adding any digits. Swapping day and month may leave the complete sum unchanged while changing the birth number. Writing the month in words resolves this easily. Reliable arithmetic begins with an unambiguous record, not with an impressive interpretation.

4 May gives birth number 4; 5 April gives 5. Both day-month sums equal 9.

Ask for the month in words when the numeric format is ambiguous. “4 May” removes a common source of confusion. Swapping day and month can leave their combined sum unchanged, so an identical full-date number does not validate the original entry. The day-based birth number changes because the day changed. Confirm the record before calculating. This is a useful example of why a correct-looking final result can conceal incorrect input. Validate the date format and the arithmetic separately instead of treating one matching number as proof that everything is right.

Matching full-date totals do not prove that the date format was correct.
