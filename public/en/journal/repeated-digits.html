Count repeated digits before interpreting them

Published: 17 October 2026

AstroVedansh Journal·158 words

Begin repeated-number analysis with an accurate count. Mark every occurrence taken from the raw date, then transfer that count to the grid. If reduced numbers are added later under a declared method, distinguish them so they are not mistaken for additional raw birth-date digits.

In the stated raw grid for 23-09-1996, 9 occurs twice and 3 once.

Separate presence from frequency. In this list, nine is present with a count of two; three is present with a count of one. Use separate columns if that makes the distinction clearer. Later, when studying a combination, check whether its stated condition concerns mere presence or repetition. Switching between those conditions without saying so creates confusion. Counting accurately is necessary, but it does not make an interpretation certain. Frequency is a property of the recorded digits; claims about personality or future outcomes belong to a separate traditional interpretive step.

Two occurrences do not establish twice the outcome or twice the ability.
