What is Lagna? Identify the ascendant before reading houses

Published: 17 October 2026

AstroVedansh Journal·170 words

Lagna, or the ascendant, is the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the birth moment within the chosen calculation system. It provides a reference for reading the chart. It is not automatically the same as the Moon sign.

When someone says “my sign is Scorpio,” ask what they mean. They may be referring to an ascendant, Moon sign or a different convention. Treating that statement as a confirmed ascendant can shift the whole reading before any interpretation begins.

Find the ascendant marker in your teaching chart and write its sign separately from the Moon’s sign. They may match, but they do not have to. Next, record the house sequence using the chart format and method you are studying. If the teacher switches to a Moon-based reference, start a separately labelled table.

A useful exercise is to explain which reference supports each observation. Do not silently change reference points halfway through a reading. Clear labels prevent an impressive-looking collection of statements from becoming a confusing mixture of different perspectives.
