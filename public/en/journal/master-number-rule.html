Should 11 remain 11 or reduce to 2?

Published: 17 October 2026

AstroVedansh Journal·154 words

Before applying a master-number convention, identify the method being taught. Some systems retain numbers such as 11, while a single-digit method reduces them further. State the chosen rule at the beginning and apply it consistently rather than changing it to obtain a preferred interpretation.

29 → 2+9=11; under a single-digit convention, 1+1=2.

State the convention before comparing answers. Some numerology approaches retain selected compound values, while others reduce to a single digit. Records from those approaches can look contradictory when their labels are missing. Write down whether your exercise retains such numbers or uses full reduction, then follow that rule consistently. Do not stop reducing one date and continue reducing another merely because you prefer the resulting interpretation. The disagreement may concern the chosen convention rather than a mathematical error. Consistency makes the calculation understandable even to someone following a different tradition.

Do not call 11 or 2 incorrect before checking the stated convention.
