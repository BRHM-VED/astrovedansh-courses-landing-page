Dashas and timing

Mahadasha and Antardasha: understand the two levels

AstroVedansh Journal·178 words

Updated: 17 October 2026

Mahadasha and Antardasha describe different levels within a dasha timing structure. Memorising their names is less useful than understanding how a larger period and its subdivisions fit together. Start by reading the table, not by attaching an event to it.

Draw one long line for the main period and smaller sections inside it. Copy the labels and dates from your teaching example. A book and its chapters provide a simple analogy: a chapter belongs within the larger work. The analogy explains structure; it does not promise a particular outcome.

Check the start and end of every section. If a date appears at several levels, identify which row represents which level rather than selecting one planet’s name in isolation. Keep calculation notes separate from your interpretation notes so that errors can be located.

The practical exercise is to reproduce one period accurately and explain its subdivisions aloud. Do not read a small section as though its surrounding context had vanished. Once the structure is clear, follow your teacher’s method for studying relationships and limitations before attempting any timing discussion.
