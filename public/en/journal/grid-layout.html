Why the grid layout must be declared first

AstroVedansh Journal·157 words

Updated: 17 October 2026

Check the empty grid before entering a birth date. This course uses 3, 1, 9 across the top; 6, 7, 5 in the middle; and 2, 8, 4 at the bottom. Interpretations belonging to another layout should not be silently transferred to these rows.

Place 5 in the middle-right cell and 7 in the centre.

The declared course layout is 3, 1, 9 across the top; 6, 7, 5 in the middle; and 2, 8, 4 at the bottom. Keep that arrangement visible while locating digits. If another source uses a different centre, first check its method instead of assuming an error. Follow the stated layout consistently for this exercise. Do not begin interpreting lines or combinations before you can construct the diagram correctly. The next question is which information belongs in it: original date digits, calculated numbers, or a separately labelled additional layer.

This is the declared course grid, not a universal layout for all numerology.
