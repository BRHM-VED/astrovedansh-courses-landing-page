Why the birth number uses only the day

Published: 17 October 2026

AstroVedansh Journal·153 words

Learn the birth number and the full-date number as separate calculations. In this Indian convention, the birth number reduces the day of birth. Adding the month and year answers a different question. Establish the calculation before considering its traditional interpretation.

23 September 1996: birth number 2 + 3 = 5.

Before adding anything, write the name of the calculation at the top of your page. Under “Mulank,” copy only the day. Reduce its digits if necessary; a day already represented by a single digit stays as it is. In this example the birth number is five, while the full-date calculation produces another result. Keep separate boxes for them. That small habit prevents later confusion about which number supports a traditional interpretation. A correct sum is only useful when it answers the intended question. Calculation and interpretation should remain separate steps.

The sum of the entire birth date is not the day-based birth number.
