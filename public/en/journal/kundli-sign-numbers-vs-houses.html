Kundli numbers explained: sign numbers are not house numbers

AstroVedansh Journal·175 words

Updated: 17 October 2026

A number written inside a North Indian rashi chart usually identifies a zodiac sign, not the number of that house. This small distinction is responsible for many beginner errors. Read the chart format before interpreting the figures.

For example, an 8 written in the first-house position indicates Scorpio there. It does not turn that position into the eighth house. The location supplies one piece of information and the sign number supplies another. If you merge them, subsequent house counting can go wrong.

Take a blank teaching chart. Mark the houses in one colour and the sign numbers in another. Then copy the same information into two separate columns. Once the difference feels clear, repeat the exercise without colour. That checks understanding rather than dependence on a visual aid.

Do not assume every chart format uses the same visual arrangement. A different regional layout may organise the information differently. Before reading an unfamiliar chart, identify its conventions and verify a few known placements. Accurate chart reading begins with this ordinary-looking step, not with a dramatic prediction.
