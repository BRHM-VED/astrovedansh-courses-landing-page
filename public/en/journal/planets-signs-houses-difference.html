Planets, signs and houses: the first distinction in reading a kundli

Published: 17 October 2026

AstroVedansh Journal·173 words

A planet, its zodiac sign and its house answer three different questions. Mixing them is one reason a chart can look familiar while remaining difficult to read. Before attempting an interpretation, describe the placement correctly.

Try this sentence: “The Moon is in Taurus and, in this example, in the second house.” Moon identifies the planet, Taurus identifies the sign, and second identifies the house. These are not interchangeable labels. In a different ascendant framework, Taurus can belong to a different house. The sign name has not changed; the reference has.

Draw a table with separate columns for planet, sign and house. Copy the placements from a teaching chart, then ask another learner to check them. Do not add predictions yet. If the lists disagree, resolve the reading error before discussing meanings.

The useful question is not “What does this placement promise?” but “Have I identified the placement accurately?” A tidy explanation built on an incorrectly read chart still starts from the wrong information. Get this distinction comfortable before moving to combinations or timing.
