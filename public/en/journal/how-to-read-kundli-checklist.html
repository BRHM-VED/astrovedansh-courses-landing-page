How to read a kundli: a beginner’s checklist

AstroVedansh Journal·178 words

Updated: 17 October 2026

Before looking for a striking combination, check whether you have read the basic chart correctly. A beginner’s kundli checklist should organise observation before interpretation. It is a working aid, not a device that guarantees predictions.

Start with the birth details, chart format and calculation context. Identify the ascendant, then list planets, signs and houses separately. Only after this information is clear should you choose a specific learning question. Trying to describe someone’s entire life immediately makes it difficult to notice missing information.

Use one teaching chart and tick each completed step. Finish by writing two short lists: what is clear and what still needs checking. If the initial placement table contains an error, resolve it before discussing marriage, work or timing. Later reasoning cannot repair an incorrect starting observation.

Review the checklist after several exercises. Reorder steps that repeatedly get missed, but keep the distinction between recorded information and interpretation. The goal is not to fill every box with an answer. It is to make your reading process clear enough that someone else can follow and question it.
