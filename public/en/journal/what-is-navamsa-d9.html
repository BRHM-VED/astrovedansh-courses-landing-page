What is Navamsa? Build your D1 foundation before D9

AstroVedansh Journal·179 words

Updated: 17 October 2026

Navamsa, commonly labelled D9, is a divisional-chart topic in traditional Vedic astrology. Beginners often open it before they can reliably read the basic birth chart. An additional diagram does not automatically produce a deeper interpretation.

First, make an accurate placement table for the main chart. Then read your chosen source’s explanation of why Navamsa is being used. Keep its observations on a separate page. If you cannot explain the question that the additional chart addresses, return to the foundational exercise rather than adding more techniques.

For one example, describe the main-chart information before writing the D9 observations. Ask what the new layer contributes and which connecting rule your teacher has actually introduced. Do not invent a relationship simply because the same planet appears in both diagrams.

The aim is not to make your notes look advanced. It is to make every step explainable. Uncertain birth details also remain uncertain when another chart is opened. Treat divisional charts as part of a structured traditional method, with prerequisites and limits, rather than a shortcut to definite answers about a person’s life.
