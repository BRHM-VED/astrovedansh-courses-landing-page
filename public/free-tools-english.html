How are these calculated?

Birth number: reduce the day of birth to 1–9. Full-date number: add every digit in the entire date, including all year digits, then reduce to 1–9. This method also reduces 11, 22 and 33.

Grid order: 3–1–9 / 6–7–5 / 2–8–4. In this AstroVedansh practice method, omit zeros and the leading 19 for birth years 1900–1999. For years from 2000 onward, use all nonzero year digits. Do not add the calculated birth or full-date numbers back into this raw grid.

Other numerology traditions may use different grids or conventions.

Sun and Moon signs: Astronomy Engine planetary positions with an approximate date-adjusted Lahiri offset. The correct birthplace UTC offset is essential. This tool does not calculate the ascendant or houses.
