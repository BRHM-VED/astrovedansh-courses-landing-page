Our editorial approach

This is the AstroVedansh study journal, published by the institute founded by Vedansh Vallabh. It explains traditional astrology and numerology methods through clear examples and responsible language.

AI assistance was used in preparing, structuring and editing these articles. No article is represented as personally reviewed by Vedansh Vallabh, or as a real consultation story, unless that is verified. Worked examples are educational; an illustrative scenario is not a client testimonial.

Arithmetic and traditional interpretation are distinct. A sum can be checked, but this does not scientifically establish predictive claims. Articles do not replace medical, legal or personal investment advice.

The images are original AI-generated conceptual illustrations, not documentary photographs of ancient events, students or achievements. Four topic-based covers are reused across relevant articles.

Send corrections or suggestions to vedanshvallabh@gmail.com. Material corrections will be reflected in the affected article’s modification date.
