About me

Hi, I'm Vedansh Vallabh.

Astrologer · Numerologist · Founder, AstroVedansh

From my spiritual roots in Hathras to life in Vrindavan, astrology has been a journey of curiosity and continuous study. Through AstroVedansh, I teach it in a simple, practical and fear-free way.

Read more about my journey

From tradition to study

My father is a Katha Vachak. Mantras, spiritual discourse and devotion were part of my upbringing. Around 2016, my interest in astrology grew into focused study.

Still a student

I completed Jyotish Praveen and Jyotish Visharad at ICAS. More than 50 astrology books, guidance from teachers and practice with real charts have shaped my understanding. I studied Chara Dasha, Yogini Dasha and divisional charts with V. P. Goel and also learned from Umang Taneja.

How I teach

I want students to understand the reasoning behind a principle, practise with examples and ask questions freely. My aim is to teach astrology with context, judgement and responsibility, without fear or incomplete information. I still consider myself a student.

Explore the full 12-week syllabus ＋
MONTH 01WEEKS 1–4

Reading a kundli

An introduction to planets, signs and houses

01How a birth chart is structured+

The D1 birth chart, ascendant, signs versus houses, degrees, and identifying planets and house rulers in a North Indian chart.

Practice: Identify the key elements in 10 blank charts.

02Understanding the nine planets+

From the Sun to Ketu: planetary nature, elements, qualities, significations and the life themes associated with each.

Practice: Map each planet's significations and record your chart observations.

03The nature of the 12 signs+

Aries to Pisces: elements, movable, fixed and dual signs, sign rulers, and how a sign influences a planet's expression.

Practice: Complete a worksheet on elements, nature and rulership.

04Houses 1–4 and your first chart lab+

Houses linked to personality, wealth, speech, family, courage, skills, mother and comfort. Begin reading the house, its ruler and its significator together.

Checkpoint: A foundation test and practice on 10 charts.

By the end of this monthIdentify planets, signs, houses and house lords in a chart.

MONTH 02WEEKS 5–8

Connecting the placements

The remaining houses, planetary strength and connections

05Understanding houses 5–8+

Houses associated with education, intellect, children, love, service, debt, marriage, partnerships and transformation.

Practice: Read the house, its ruler and significator together in eight charts.

06Houses 9–12 and their rulers+

Fortune, teachers, action, profession, income, expenses and foreign connections. Explore how a reading changes when a house ruler occupies another house.

Practice: Create a placement table for all 12 house rulers.

07How strong is a planet?+

Exaltation, debilitation, own sign, Mooltrikona, friendly and hostile signs. An introduction to combustion, retrogression, Vargottama, degrees and directional strength.

Practice: Assess 15 planetary placements and explain your reasoning.

08Aspects, conjunctions and connections+

The seventh aspect, the special aspects of Mars, Jupiter and Saturn, conjunctions, exchange of signs and dispositor relationships.

Checkpoint: A practical test on planetary relationships.

By the end of this monthForm a basic interpretation using planetary positions and relationships.

MONTH 03WEEKS 9–12

A complete chart reading

Yogas, planetary periods, transits and final practice

09The logic behind the houses+

Angular, trinal, difficult, growth and Maraka houses; the four life aims. A simple introduction to Bhavat Bhavam, Karakat Bhavam and dispositor chains.

Practice: Classify the houses and work through Bhavat Bhavam examples.

10Recognising basic yogas+

Raj Yoga, Dhan Yoga, Dharma-Karmadhipati, Parivartan, Neecha Bhanga and Vipreet Raj Yoga. Understand the difference between a yoga being present and its results being realised.

Practice: Identify yogas and the houses and planets involved in 20 charts.

11An introduction to periods and transits+

Vimshottari major and sub-periods, and the houses of the period ruler. An introduction to major transits of Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu, using the natal chart and Moon as references.

Practice: Create a basic profile of your current major and sub-period rulers.

12The seven-step reading and final practical+

Practise reading personality, education, profession, wealth and relationship tendencies in five guided charts. Finish with an unfamiliar chart, an oral assessment and revision.

Final task: Write a two-to-three-page basic analysis of an unfamiliar D1 chart.

By the end of this monthWrite a basic birth-chart reading and explain your reasoning.
