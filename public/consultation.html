BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few things you may be wondering.

Which session should I choose?

Choose 30 minutes for one main topic, or 60 minutes for a more detailed discussion. If unsure, share your question and ask the team to help you choose before booking.

Will I speak with Vedansh or a team astrologer?

The Expert astrology option is for a team astrologer. For other options, ask the team to confirm the astrologer before you pay. The founder’s profile does not mean every session is personally conducted by him.

What is the consultation fee?

The team will confirm the fee for your selected option before you proceed. Online Razorpay payment is not active on this page yet.

Can I choose the time?

Choose your preferred day, at least two days from today. The team will coordinate your session time on WhatsApp and confirm availability.

Can astrology guarantee an outcome?

Astrology offers a traditional perspective for reflection. Your circumstances and actions matter, and a consultation does not guarantee an event or outcome.
